# Hearth & Grain

Palette: #304B3B, #5C7A6A, #C9AD7F, #F5EFE4, #2B2B28

Typography: Headings: Fraunces (serif, warm, slightly grainy texture suits the name). Body: system sans-serif stack for offline reliability and clarity. Heading scale ~2rem on small screens; body 1rem with 1.6 line height. Weight contrast (600 headings, 400 body) replaces extra fonts to keep the bundle light.

Warm restraint: earthy deep green and toasted grain tones, minimal accents, generous whitespace — nothing loud.

Legible first: body text meets WCAG AA 4.5:1 contrast; dark ink text on light parchment surfaces throughout.

Honest boundaries: visuals reflect a digital planning practice only — no imagery implying cooking, premises, or delivery.

Quiet craft: simple serif headings, clean line detail, and consistent spacing evoke handmade care without clutter.
